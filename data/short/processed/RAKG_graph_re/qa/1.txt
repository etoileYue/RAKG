[Q1] 蝴蝶的生命周期包括哪四个主要阶段？
答案：蝴蝶的生命周期包括四个主要阶段：蛋（Egg）、幼虫（Caterpillar）、蛹（Pupa）和成虫（Adult Butterfly）。
证据来源：
1. [entity:Butterfly.attribute:Life Cycle Stages] Butterfly 的属性 Life Cycle Stages: Egg, Larva (Caterpillar), Pupa (Chrysalis), Adult
图谱路径：
1. Host Plant <-[Lays Eggs On]-- Butterfly

[Q2] 雌性蝴蝶通常把卵产在哪里？为什么？
答案：雌性蝴蝶通常把卵产在植物的叶子上，因为叶子为卵和孵化后的幼虫提供了栖息地和保护。
证据来源：
1. [entity:female butterfly.description] The adult stage of a butterfly that lays eggs on host plants to begin the life cycle.
2. [entity:leaves.attribute:Function in Butterfly Life Cycle] Provide a surface for egg-laying and a habitat for caterpillars
3. [entity:leaves.attribute:Location for Egg Placement] Underside
4. [entity:leaves.attribute:Role in Protection] Offer protection from predators
图谱路径：
1. female butterfly <-[Host Plant Part]-- leaves

[Q3] 毛毛虫在幼虫阶段的主要活动是什么？
答案：毛毛虫在幼虫阶段的主要活动是进食和生长，为形成蛹做好准备。
证据来源：
1. [entity:caterpillar.description] The larval stage of a butterfly, which feeds and grows before forming a chrysalis.
图谱路径：
1. stem --[Attaches To]-> caterpillar

[Q4] 在蛹阶段，毛毛虫会发生什么变化？
答案：在蛹阶段，毛毛虫会经历一种称为变态的过程，其器官会分解成一种类似汤状的物质，然后形成新的组织和结构，最终发育成成年蝴蝶。
证据来源：
1. [entity:pupa.description] The pupa is a stage in the life cycle of a butterfly during which the caterpillar undergoes a remarkable transformation known as metamorphosis inside a protective shell called a chrysalis.
2. [entity:pupa.attribute:Process] Metamorphosis
3. [entity:Insects.attribute:Development Process] Eggs hatch, organs dissolve into a soupy substance, new tissues and structures form
图谱路径：
1. larva <-[followed by]-- egg
2. larva <-[Follows]-- pupa

[Q5] 成虫蝴蝶主要吃什么食物？它们在生态系统中有什么作用？
答案：成虫蝴蝶主要以花蜜为食，它们通过吸食花蜜帮助植物传粉，从而在生态系统中起到重要的传粉作用，同时有助于维持植物种群的平衡。
证据来源：
1. [entity:Butterfly.attribute:Feeding Habits] Adults feed on nectar from flowers using a proboscis; caterpillars feed on leaves
2. [entity:Butterfly.attribute:Role in Ecosystem] Important for pollination and maintaining plant population balance
图谱路径：
1. Leaves <-[Eats]-- Butterfly
2. Adult Butterfly Stage <-[Includes]-- State
